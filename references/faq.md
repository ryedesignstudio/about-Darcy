Yes. She's empowering and thoughtful, loves mentoring, and builds community wherever she goes. She led an in-person Claude Skills workshop for 200+ people, and at Amazon she hosted design events, managed speakers, and organized volunteering events.

**What is she like as a direct report?**
Low risk, high impact, and easy to trust. She:
- Shares her thinking clearly and often, so her manager is never guessing where things stand.
- Is proactive. She spots what needs doing and moves on it without being asked.
- Responds quickly when someone needs something from her.
- Treats people with kindness and respect.

**What is she looking for in her next role?**
1. **Growth in AI product design.** A role where she helps define how agentic AI behaves and is experienced, not one where she applies someone else's patterns. She also wants room to keep being an AI UX thought leader.
2. **A strong UX culture.** A team that understands design's value and protects the work, with UX leadership that brings design in early and advocates for its influence across teams.
3. **Scale of impact.** Work that reaches a lot of people.
4. **Partners and leaders she can grow under.** Close collaboration with an aligned product and engineering team, and with UX teammates who are dedicated to the work full time.
5. **A clear line of sight to shipping.** Concrete ideas a team is building together. Long-range vision work is great when a team owns it and it connects to what ships.
6. **A high bar for craft.** A team where taste and attention to detail are the norm.

A few more things that matter to her:
- **Users drive the roadmap.** She wants what she makes to be valuable to the people using it.
- **Real knowledge of the users.** Either building on her existing domain expertise, or joining a v1 project with a team that learns the space together.
- **Room to learn.** She'd rather stretch than sit somewhere because it's comfortable.
- **Community.** She wants to use her knowledge and network to make UX better for all practitioners.
