# What It's Like to Work With Darcy

## Who she is as a designer

Darcy calls herself a **Humanist Technologist**. She cares about the emotional texture of technology: how it feels to use, not just whether it works. Her goal is to make complex systems feel simple, human, and safe.

Her background is a mix that shows up in her work. She studied biology with an HCI focus, got FP&A certified to understand finance users, and designs AI products for people who need to trust the output.

## Who she is as a person

People describe Darcy as **empowering and thoughtful**. She lifts the people around her and takes care with how her work and words land.

- **Loves mentoring.** She makes time for other designers and enjoys helping them grow.
- **Builds communities.** She brings people together around design and AI, and keeps them coming back.
- **Teaches what she learns.** She led an in-person Claude Skills workshop for 200+ people.
- **Hosts and organizes.** At Amazon she hosted design events, managed speakers, and organized volunteering events.

On a team, this means she raises the level of the people around her, not just her own work.

## Her three strengths

1. **Ferocious learner.** She goes deep on unfamiliar domains fast. When she designed for finance analysts, she earned an FP&A certification so she could think like them.
2. **World-class craft.** High polish, real attention to detail, and moments of delight (like making AI wait time feel fun).
3. **Systems at scale.** Comfortable with messy, complex products that serve thousands of users with different needs.

Empathy and HCI sit underneath all three.

## How she works

- **Prototypes to think.** She uses AI-native tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Claude Design) to build working prototypes and validate ideas quickly. She calls it vibe coding. It's for learning and proving ideas, not shipping production code. Teams get something real to react to early, instead of a slide.
- **Designs AI behavior, not just screens.** She thinks about how an agent should act, explain itself, and earn trust.
- **Designs for each audience.** One product, different views for different people (analysts vs. executives).
- **Speaks in outcomes.** She tracks the impact of her work and ties design decisions to numbers.
- **Clear, warm communicator.** Plain language, direct, no jargon walls.

## What her past panels said

Interviewers have called out two things in particular: designing for agentic AI, and systems thinking through complexity.

## Where she does her best work

- On teams helping **define how agentic AI behaves and is experienced**, not just applying patterns someone else set.
- In a **strong UX culture**, with leadership that sees the value of design, brings it in early, and protects the work.
- With a **clear line of sight to shipping**, working with a team on concrete ideas.
- Where the **bar for craft is high** and the work reaches people at scale.
- She's happy designing for developers and enterprise users as well as consumers.
