## 1. AskRino: making AI trustworthy (Amazon)

**What it is:** An AI assistant for finance teams at Amazon Devices & Services.

**The problem:** People didn't trust what the AI told them. When you can't see how an answer was made, you double check everything, and the tool stops saving time.

**What Darcy did:**
- Designed a "Glass Box" transparency pattern so users could see how the AI reached its answer, instead of treating it as a black box.
- Designed "Select Prompt," a guided entry point so people didn't face a blank text box and had a clear way to start.
- Prototyped custom "thinking" animations so waiting on the AI felt a little joyful instead of dead.

**Results:**
- AI satisfaction (CSAT) rose from 49% to 81%
- 35,000 hours of manual work removed

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest example of how Darcy thinks about agentic AI. She doesn't just put a chat box on a product. She designs how the AI behaves, how it explains itself, and how it makes people feel safe relying on it.

## 2. Odyssey, "From Maze to Map" (Amazon)

**What it is:** An enterprise planning platform for large portfolio decisions, used by 10,000+ people.

**The problem:** Finance data lived in a maze of tables and manually built charts. Executives needed a clear picture to make investment calls, and analysts were spending hours building it.

**What Darcy did:**
- Turned Amazon Devices finance data into dashboards executives used for investment decisions.
- Designed separate views for different people: analysts got the underlying numbers, VPs got the decision.
- Built a roadmap "source of truth" view and a row-level drill-in view.

**Results:**
- 62% less manual chart building
- 50+ hours reclaimed every month
- 40% efficiency gain in the drill-in view
- 89% of users preferred the new graphs over tables

**Read more:** https://www.ryedesign.studio/financial-planning

## 3. iTrustCapital: AI onboarding (consumer fintech)

**What it is:** A consumer fintech that lets people invest 401k and IRA retirement savings in crypto. Many users were everyday people, not investors, and a lot of them were seniors.

**Darcy's role:** Design leader. She led the company's first mobile app launch.

**The problem:** Long, regulated government forms. This was exactly where people gave up.

**What Darcy did:** Pitched and designed an AI onboarding flow. Users scan their ID and the required forms fill themselves in.

**Results:**
- Users grew from 2,000 to 35,000 within two months of launch (17x)
- Onboarding became 5x faster
- Support calls dropped 30%

**Why it matters:** Shows she can design for regular people, under regulation, in a high-trust space like retirement money.
